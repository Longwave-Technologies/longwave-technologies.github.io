Copier Sales, Leasing & Repair in NYC, Long Island & Westchester

Longwave Technologies sells, leases, and repairs new and affordable used (refurbished) black & white and color copiers and multifunction printers for small and medium-sized businesses in New York City, Long Island, and Westchester. We also supply toner and copier parts. Family business serving the Tri-State area since 1991.

Our Services

  • Copier sales (new and used): New and affordable used (refurbished) black & white and color copiers and multifunction printers (MFPs) that print, copy, scan, and fax.
  • Copier leasing and rentals: Copier leases and flexible equipment rental agreements, so you don't have to pay the full price up front.

  • Toner and copier supplies: Original and compatible (generic) toner cartridges, parts, and supplies.
  • Delivery, installation, and setup: Delivery and installation across the Tri-State area, plus network, printer, and scanner setup for Windows and Mac.
